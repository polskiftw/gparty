FLTK's bundled NanoSVG fork is modified for FLTK. Its parser notes that it is
based on the Anti-Grain Geometry 2.4 SVG example (Copyright (C) 2002-2004
Maxim Shemanarev), with arc calculation based on canvg. Its rasterizer notes
that polygon rasterization is heavily based on stb_truetype by Sean Barrett.
