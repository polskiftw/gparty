SQLite Public Domain Notice
===========================

The SQLite core library is in the public domain. Its authors have dedicated
their SQLite contributions to the public domain, and no license is required to
use the SQLite core library.

Anyone may copy, modify, publish, use, compile, sell, or distribute the original
SQLite code, either in source form or as a compiled binary, for any purpose,
commercial or non-commercial, and by any means.

SQLite's traditional blessing is:

May you do good and not evil.
May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others.
May you share freely, never taking more than you give.

This file is a self-contained public-domain notice rather than a pointer to an
external license. SQLite has no separate license text because the core library
is public domain.
